[2025-09-29 14:09] @david.le
Nobody starts over, and we do not edit the 25 in place. The new schema ubo_4tier_lookthrough v2.0.0 replaces the table with a recursive repeater (L41); an entity's children only open when it meets the threshold and sits at Tier 1-3 (L58).
The threshold becomes ubo_threshold_pct (L11), default 25, resolved per investor by rule ubo.resolve_threshold (L69): if investor.strict_lookthrough (L36) is true it takes strict_lookthrough_threshold_pct = 10 (L13). Who is in scope is a toggle, strict_lookthrough_applies_to (L14), and depth is max_depth = 4 (L15).

[2025-09-29 14:15] @david.le
How we change the threshold without breaking anyone's draft:
1. Drafts are pinned to the schema version they started on, so publishing v2 does not touch them by itself.
2. migrate.v1_to_v2 (L81) maps every v1 row into the repeater at its tier, keeps every value already entered, then re-runs ubo.resolve_threshold.
3. Only drafts that gain new required rows go to NEEDS_INFO, and the email lists just those rows. Everyone else stays exactly as they were.
4. Signed subscriptions are out of scope for the migration.
If we flipped 25 to 10 in place, every half-filled strict-scope draft would fail validation on its next save with no explanation. That is the restart we are avoiding.
