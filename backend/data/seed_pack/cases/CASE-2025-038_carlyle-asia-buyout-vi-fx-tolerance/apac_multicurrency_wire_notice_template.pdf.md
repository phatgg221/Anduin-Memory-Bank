## Section 3. Intermediary Routing by Remitting Currency

Most delays in CASE-2025-038 came from wires routed through intermediaries that were not on the notice. If your bank converts from the currencies below, ask it to route the U.S. dollar leg through the listed correspondent (field 56A) where it has that relationship.

| Remitting currency | Typical remitter | Conversion point | Field 56A intermediary (USD leg) | Settlement window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| JPY | Japanese insurers, pension funds, regional banks | Remitter's bank, Tokyo | Remitter's own New York branch or USD correspondent, if any; otherwise [Fund's bank] direct | T+1 to T+2 |
| EUR | Continental European pension funds and insurers | Remitter's bank, eurozone | Remitter's USD correspondent in New York | T+1 to T+2 |
| GBP | UK pension schemes | Remitter's bank, London | Remitter's USD correspondent in New York | T+1 |

## Section 4. FX Settlement Tolerance and True-Up

(a) Under the FX Settlement Tolerance Policy adopted by the General Partner under Section [3.4(e)] of the LPA, a contribution received within the tolerance band in Section 1 is treated as paid in full.
(b) Any difference within the band is recorded in the Limited Partner's true-up ledger and added to, or credited against, its next Capital Contribution. Overpayments below USD 1,000 are credited, not refunded.
(c) A contribution outside the band is a shortfall. The General Partner may issue a notice under Section [3.4(a)] of the LPA, and the default provisions of the LPA apply.
