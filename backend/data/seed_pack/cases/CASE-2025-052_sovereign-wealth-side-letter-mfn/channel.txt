[2025-08-25 17:05] @david.le
Expected. Sovereigns usually can't give an open-ended indemnity: statutory immunity, and sometimes their own constitutive law, gets in the way. Don't fight the immunity. Reserve it, and carve the commercial obligations out of the reservation.
Whether an FSIA exception (28 U.S.C. §1605(a)(1)-(2)) would apply is a question for a court. The GP's real protection is remedies exercised inside the partnership (default remedies, set-off against distributions), and those need no court.

[2025-09-02 11:48] @david.le
That's the trap. An untagged clause is an Electable Provision, so every LP at US$25M or more could elect "no indemnity" through the MFN. Any concession granted because of sovereign status has to be tagged as an Excluded Provision.
I'll draft from our clause library tonight: an immunity reservation, plus set-off and a cap in place of the indemnity, both excluded.
