# Subscription Agreement — Silverpine Heritage Cayman Feeder IV, L.P.
*Excerpt: Sections 3 and 4, Annex B and Schedule A · Final form circulated 21 March 2025*

Excerpt from the subscription agreement (the **"Agreement"**) between Silverpine Heritage Cayman Feeder IV, L.P., a Cayman Islands exempted limited partnership registered with the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (**"CIMA"**) under the Private Funds Act (as revised) (the **"Partnership"**), acting by its general partner (the **"General Partner"**), and the subscriber named on the Investor Signature Page (the **"Subscriber"**). The Partnership invests substantially all of its assets through Silverpine Heritage Master Fund IV, L.P. (the **"Master Fund"**). Capitalised terms not defined herein are defined in Section 1 of the Agreement.

## Section 3. Anti-Money Laundering and Investor Identification

### 3.1 Applicable Requirements
The Subscriber acknowledges that the Partnership is subject to the Proceeds of Crime Act (as revised), the Anti-Money Laundering Regulations (as revised) of the Cayman Islands (the **"AML Regulations"**) and the guidance notes issued by CIMA (together, the **"AML Requirements"**). The Partnership has delegated its customer due diligence procedures to Harbourline Fund Services (Cayman) Ltd. (the **"Administrator"**), without relieving the Partnership of responsibility for compliance with the AML Requirements.

### 3.2 Identification and Verification
Before acceptance of its subscription, and thereafter on request, the Subscriber shall provide all information and documents that the General Partner or the Administrator reasonably requires to identify and verify:
(a) the Subscriber, its legal form, constitutional documents, registered office, directors and signatories;
(b) each natural person who ultimately owns or controls, directly or indirectly, twenty-five per cent (25%) or more of the interests or voting rights in the Subscriber, or who otherwise controls its management (each, a **"Beneficial Owner"**), looking through each intermediate holding entity;
(c) the source of funds for each Capital Contribution and, for a Subscriber assessed as higher risk, the source of wealth of each Beneficial Owner; and
(d) the authority of each Joint Signatory (as defined in Section 4.3) to bind the Subscriber.
> Drafting note: the 25% look-through in Section 3.2(b) is a fund-level policy setting specified by the General Partner, not a threshold prescribed by the AML Regulations.

### 3.3 AML Officers; Refusal and Suspension
The Partnership has appointed an Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (the **"AML Compliance Officer"**), a Money Laundering Reporting Officer (the **"MLRO"**) and a Deputy MLRO in accordance with the AML Regulations. The MLRO may be obliged to report suspicion of money laundering, terrorist financing or proliferation financing to the Financial Reporting Authority of the Cayman Islands, and no such report shall breach any duty of confidentiality owed to the Subscriber. The General Partner may, in consultation with the AML Compliance Officer, refuse a subscription, suspend distributions or apply enhanced due diligence (including a lower look-through threshold) where the Subscriber fails to comply with Section 3.2 or is assessed as higher risk.

## Section 4. Tax Matters: FATCA and CRS

### 4.1 Tax Certifications
The Subscriber shall deliver, in the form set out in Schedule A, IRS Form W-8BEN-E and a CRS entity self-certification or, if it is a U.S. person exempt from tax under Section 501(a) of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (the **"Code"**), IRS Form W-9 and evidence of such exemption (the **"Tax Certifications"**).

### 4.2 Cayman Islands Reporting
The Partnership reports under the Tax Information Authority Act (as revised) and the regulations thereunder implementing the Cayman Islands–United States intergovernmental agreement in respect of Chapter 4 of the Code (**"FATCA"**) and the OECD Common Reporting Standard (the **"CRS"**). The Administrator may report information on the Subscriber, its substantial U.S. owners and its Controlling Persons to the Cayman Islands Tax Information Authority (the **"TIA"**) via the DITC Portal, for exchange with the U.S. Internal Revenue Service and other competent authorities.

### 4.3 Joint Signatories
(a) Where the Subscriber's constitutional documents require execution by two or more authorised persons acting jointly (each, a **"Joint Signatory"**, and the account, a **"Joint Account"**), each Joint Signatory shall be identified in Annex B.
(b) Notwithstanding Section 4.3(a), the Subscriber shall designate in Annex B exactly one Joint Signatory (the **"Designated Tax Signatory"**) who alone shall: (i) select the Chapter 4 status in Part I, line 5 of IRS Form W-8BEN-E and complete the corresponding Part of that form; (ii) complete Part XXIX (Substantial U.S. Owners of Passive NFFE), where applicable; (iii) make the CRS entity classification and identify any Controlling Persons; and (iv) sign Part XXX (Certification) of IRS Form W-8BEN-E and the CRS self-certification. No other Joint Signatory may make, amend or withdraw any such election, and the Partnership may rely conclusively on the Designated Tax Signatory's elections.
(c) Each other Joint Signatory shall review the Tax Certifications and sign the acknowledgement in paragraph 3 of Annex B, which is not a signature of IRS Form W-8BEN-E.
(d) On the Partnership's electronic subscription platform, the Tax Certifications are routed first to the Designated Tax Signatory and the Annex B acknowledgements thereafter, and entries made by any Joint Signatory have no effect until submitted by the Designated Tax Signatory.
> Drafting note: Section 4.3(b) is a Partnership-specific allocation of authority, not a requirement of FATCA, the CRS or Cayman Islands law.

### 4.4 Substantial U.S. Owners and Controlling Persons
If the Designated Tax Signatory certifies the Subscriber as a passive NFFE (Part XXVI), the Subscriber shall either certify that it has no substantial U.S. owners (line 40b) or report the name, address and U.S. TIN of each substantial U.S. owner in Part XXIX (line 40c). "Substantial U.S. owner" has the meaning given in Section 1473(2) of the Code. A CRS Passive NFE shall also self-certify each Controlling Person.

### 4.5 Change in Circumstances; Failure to Certify
Within thirty (30) days of any change that renders a Tax Certification inaccurate, the Subscriber shall deliver an update signed by the Designated Tax Signatory, who may be replaced only by notice signed by all Joint Signatories with an updated Annex B. On any failure, the General Partner may allocate resulting withholding to the Subscriber or compulsorily withdraw it.
