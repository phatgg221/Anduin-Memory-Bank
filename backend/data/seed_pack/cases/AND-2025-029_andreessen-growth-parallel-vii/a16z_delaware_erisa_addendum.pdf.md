# Andreessen Growth Fund VII: Main and Parallel Partnerships
*Subscription Booklet, Addendum C (Form of June 2025). Excerpt: Sections 2, 3, 4 and 6; Schedule A*

This Addendum C forms part of each Subscription Agreement for limited partner interests (the "**Interests**") in Andreessen Growth Fund VII, L.P. (the "**Main Fund**") or Andreessen Growth Parallel Fund VII, L.P. (the "**Parallel Fund**" and, with the Main Fund, the "**Partnerships**"), Delaware limited partnerships that invest side by side and whose general partner is AGF VII GP, L.L.C. (the "**General Partner**"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the applicable Limited Partnership Agreement (each, an "**LPA**").

## Section 2. Delaware Limited Partnership Representations

**2.1 Authority.** The Subscriber, if an entity, is duly organized and in good standing, and its signatory is authorized to bind it.

**2.2 Admission.** The Subscriber will be admitted as a limited partner under the Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act (6 *Del. C.* §17-101 *et seq.*) only upon acceptance of its subscription, in whole or in part, by the General Partner; no subscription binds a Partnership until countersigned.

**2.3 Investment Intent; Qualified Purchaser.** The Subscriber acquires the Interests, which are not registered under the Securities Act of 1933, for its own account and not with a view to distribution, and is a "qualified purchaser" under Section 2(a)(51) of the Investment Company Act of 1940.

## Section 3. Accredited Investor Status; Rule 506(c) Verification

**3.1 Representation.** The Subscriber is an "accredited investor" as defined in Rule 501(a) of Regulation D. The Interests are offered in reliance on Rule 506(c), and general solicitation may have been used.

**3.2 Reasonable Steps to Verify.** Rule 506(c) requires the Partnerships to take reasonable steps to verify that each purchaser is an accredited investor. The Subscriber shall deliver one of the following, as the General Partner may request, failing which its subscription may be declined:
(a) a written confirmation, dated within the prior three months, from a registered broker-dealer, an SEC-registered investment adviser, a licensed attorney or a certified public accountant;
(b) for an entity, financial statements showing total assets over US$5,000,000; or
(c) if its Capital Commitment meets the LPA minimum, a written representation that it is accredited and that no third party financed the commitment for the purpose of acquiring the Interests.

## Section 4. ERISA Benefit Plan Investor Representations

**4.1 Status.** The Subscriber shall complete Schedule A, represents that it is accurate, and shall notify the General Partner within ten (10) Business Days of any change. "**Benefit Plan Investor**" has the meaning in Section 3(42) of ERISA: (i) an employee benefit plan subject to Part 4 of Title I of ERISA, (ii) a plan subject to Section 4975 of the Internal Revenue Code (the "**Code**"), or (iii) an entity whose underlying assets include plan assets by reason of a plan's investment in the entity.

**4.2 Plan-Asset Entities.** A Section 4.1(iii) Subscriber states on Schedule A, Line A3, the percentage of its equity held by Benefit Plan Investors and is counted only to that extent.

**4.3 Other Plans.** A governmental plan, a church plan that has not elected under Code Section 410(d), or a non-U.S. plan is not a Benefit Plan Investor, but represents that its investment does not violate any law substantially similar to Title I of ERISA or Code Section 4975 ("**Similar Law**").

## Section 6. Plan Asset Limitation; Parallel Fund Aggregation

**6.1 Significant Participation.** Under 29 C.F.R. §2510.3-101 (the "**Plan Asset Regulation**"), participation by Benefit Plan Investors in an entity is significant if, immediately after the most recent acquisition of any equity interest, they hold 25% or more of the value of any class of equity interests (§2510.3-101(f)).

**6.2 Limitation.** Each Partnership intends that its assets not constitute plan assets and will limit Benefit Plan Investor participation so that it is not significant.

**6.3 Disregarded Interests.** In applying Sections 6.1 and 6.4, the value of Interests held by the General Partner, any person with discretionary authority or control over the Partnerships' assets or that provides investment advice to them for a fee, and their affiliates, is disregarded, except to the extent held by a Benefit Plan Investor.

**6.4 Parallel Fund Aggregation.** In addition to the per-Partnership test:
(a) As a contractual covenant under each LPA, the General Partner shall monitor and manage Benefit Plan Investor participation on an **aggregate basis across the Main Fund and the Parallel Fund**, as if their limited partner Interests were a single class.
(b) Value is measured by Capital Commitments: Benefit Plan Investor Capital Commitments (weighted under Section 4.2) divided by all Capital Commitments, across both Partnerships and excluding interests disregarded under Section 6.3.
(c) The percentage is determined immediately before the General Partner accepts any subscription, increase or transfer, counting every subscription then submitted and not rejected or deferred.
(d) If acceptance would cause the aggregate percentage, or the percentage in either Partnership, to equal or exceed 25%, the General Partner may, in its sole discretion, reject the subscription, reduce the Capital Commitment accepted, or defer acceptance to a subsequent Closing, and may allocate any reduction among Benefit Plan Investors as it determines.
(e) The General Partner may manage to a lower internal limit and is not liable to any Subscriber for a reduction made in good faith under this Section 6.4.

> Drafting note: Section 6.4 aggregation is contractual; it is not required by the Plan Asset Regulation.
