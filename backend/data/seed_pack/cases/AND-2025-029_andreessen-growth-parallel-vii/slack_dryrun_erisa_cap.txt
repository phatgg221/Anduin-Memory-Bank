[2025-07-22 22:38] @qa.linh
Why READ COMMITTED at L40? I'd have expected SERIALIZABLE for something like this.

[2025-07-22 22:44] @sarah.tran
Postgres takes a repeatable-read/serializable snapshot at the first statement, which is the lock call itself, before it waits. The second waiter would wake up and count a stale book: same bug again. READ COMMITTED + the family lock gives a fresh read after the wait.
Idempotency is on the delivery id (L48): a webhook retry can't double-count, but a new GP attempt after a scale-back re-counts.
