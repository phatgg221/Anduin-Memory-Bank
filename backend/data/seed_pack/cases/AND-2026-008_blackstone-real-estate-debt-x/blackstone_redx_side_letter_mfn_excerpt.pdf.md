# Blackstone Real Estate Debt X — Side Letter and MFN Policy
*Annex C to the Partnership Agreements · Execution Version dated 15 December 2025 · Excerpt*

This Annex C forms part of the partnership agreements of Blackstone Real Estate Debt X (Master), L.P., a Cayman Islands exempted limited partnership (the "Master Fund"), and its feeder funds, Blackstone Real Estate Debt X (Delaware Feeder), L.P. and Blackstone Real Estate Debt X (Cayman Feeder), L.P. (the "Delaware Feeder" and the "Cayman Feeder"; together, the "Feeder Funds"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the applicable Partnership Agreement.

## Section 1. Eligibility for MFN Rights

### 1.1 Definitions

(a) "Affiliated Investor Group" means Limited Partners under common control or common discretionary investment management, as accepted by the General Partner under Section 1.3.
(b) "Aggregate Commitment" means the USD Equivalent of a Limited Partner's Commitment plus the USD Equivalents of the Commitments of the other members of its Affiliated Investor Group.
(c) "Submission Date" means the date (in London time) on which the Administrator receives a Limited Partner's completed Subscription Documents or Commitment increase agreement.
(d) "USD Equivalent" means the amount of a Commitment expressed in US Dollars under Section 2.3.

### 1.2 MFN Tiers

Each Limited Partner whose Aggregate Commitment is US$25,000,000 or more (an "Eligible Investor") shall be assigned an MFN Tier as follows:

(a) Tier 1: US$25,000,000 or more but less than US$50,000,000;
(b) Tier 2: US$50,000,000 or more but less than US$100,000,000; and
(c) Tier 3: US$100,000,000 or more.

Each Side Letter provision carries the MFN Tier of the Limited Partner to which it was granted or, if that Limited Partner is not an Eligible Investor, Tier 1 (its "Provision Tier"). An Eligible Investor may elect any Electable Provision whose Provision Tier is equal to or lower than its own MFN Tier.

### 1.3 Aggregation of Affiliated Commitments

Commitments of the members of an Affiliated Investor Group to either Feeder Fund are aggregated for purposes of Sections 1.2 and 3, after each is converted into its USD Equivalent under Section 2.3 at its own Reference Rate. Affiliates must be identified in the Subscription Documents. The admission of, or an increase by, any member causes the MFN Tier of every member to be redetermined.

## Section 2. Commitments and Currency

### 2.1 Fund Currency

The Master Fund and each Feeder Fund report in US Dollars, and all thresholds herein are in US Dollars.

### 2.2 Permitted Currencies

Commitments to the Delaware Feeder are denominated in US Dollars. Commitments to the Cayman Feeder may be denominated in US Dollars, Euro, Pounds Sterling or Australian Dollars (each, a "Permitted Currency"). Non-USD Commitments are called in their own currency, and related hedging costs are borne solely by their holders.

### 2.3 Conversion of Non-USD Commitments

(a) For the sole purpose of determining Aggregate Commitments, MFN Tiers and Fee Tiers, each Commitment in a Permitted Currency other than US Dollars shall be converted into US Dollars at the Reference Rate on the Rate Fixing Date.
(b) "Reference Rate" means the WM/Refinitiv 4:00 p.m. London closing spot rate (mid) for the relevant currency against the US Dollar, expressed as US Dollars per unit to four decimal places; "Rate Fixing Date" means the London business day immediately preceding the Submission Date.
(c) If no Reference Rate is published for the Rate Fixing Date, the rate last published within the preceding five London business days applies; failing that, the General Partner shall determine a commercially reasonable rate in good faith and record its basis.
(d) The USD Equivalent shall be rounded to the nearest whole US Dollar (US$0.50 rounded upward), and the thresholds in Sections 1.2 and 3.1 shall be applied to the rounded amount.
(e) An increase in a Commitment is converted at the Reference Rate for its own Rate Fixing Date, and the USD Equivalent of the existing Commitment is not restated. On a reduction or Transfer of all or part of a Commitment, its USD Equivalent is reduced in the same proportion.
(f) No MFN Tier or Fee Tier shall be reduced solely by reason of a movement in exchange rates after the applicable Rate Fixing Date.
(g) The Administrator shall record the Reference Rate, Rate Fixing Date and resulting USD Equivalent of each Commitment and each increase; that record is conclusive absent manifest error.

> Drafting note: Section 2.3 governs tier and class determination only. Capital calls and Management Fees on non-USD Commitments are unaffected.

## Section 3. Fee Tiers

### 3.1 Fee Tier Schedule

Interests are issued in the Class corresponding to the Fee Tier of the Limited Partner's Aggregate Commitment:

| Fee Tier | Aggregate Commitment | Class of Interests | Management Fee (per annum) | MFN Rights |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | Less than US$25,000,000 | Class A | 1.25% | None |
| Tier 1 | US$25,000,000 to US$49,999,999 | Class B | 1.10% | Tier 1 provisions |
| Tier 2 | US$50,000,000 to US$99,999,999 | Class C | 1.00% | Tier 1 and 2 provisions |
| Tier 3 | US$100,000,000 or more | Class D | 0.85% | All Electable Provisions |

Management Fees accrue on Invested Capital and are payable quarterly in advance. Carried Interest of 15%, subject to a 7% Preferred Return, applies to every Class.

### 3.2 Determination and Reclassification

Fee Tiers are determined on admission and redetermined on any increase, reduction or Transfer of a Commitment or any change in the Affiliated Investor Group. A reclassification takes effect from the first day of the next calendar quarter without adjustment of Management Fees already paid, and Section 2.3(f) applies to every redetermination. Management Fee reductions granted to Seed Investors or Initial Closing investors are not Fee Tiers and are Excluded Provisions under Section 4.3(c).

## Section 4. MFN Elections

### 4.1 MFN Compendium

Within 60 days after the Final Closing Date, the General Partner shall deliver to each Eligible Investor a compendium of all Side Letter provisions granted on or before that date (the "MFN Compendium"), without identifying the recipients and stating each provision's Provision Tier and whether it is an Electable Provision.

### 4.2 Election Period and Procedure

An Eligible Investor may elect any Electable Provision available to it under Section 1.2 by written notice delivered within 30 days after the MFN Compendium is delivered (the "Election Period"); notice through the electronic election form on the investor portal suffices. An elected provision is available only with every condition and obligation attached to it in the original Side Letter, and applies prospectively from the General Partner's confirmation. Provisions not elected within the Election Period are irrevocably waived.

### 4.3 Excluded Provisions

Every Side Letter provision is an "Electable Provision" except the following, which are not electable at any MFN Tier:

(a) any provision relating to a Limited Partner's particular legal, regulatory, tax or policy status, including provisions relating to ERISA, public records or freedom of information laws, tax-exempt status or unrelated business taxable income, and placement agent or pay-to-play rules;
(b) any provision granted to a sovereign wealth fund, governmental plan, central bank or supranational organization by reason of that status, including any reservation of immunity;
(c) any Management Fee, Carried Interest or expense term granted to a Seed Investor or an Initial Closing investor;
(d) any right to appoint a member of, or an observer to, the Advisory Committee;
(e) any right relating to co-investment opportunities;
(f) any provision granted to the General Partner, the Investment Manager or their Affiliates or employees; and
(g) any provision relating to governing law, jurisdiction, forum or service of process.
